Boards hold posts that stay up: announcements, notices, discussion and in-character writing. You can read and post in game or on the website.

- `+bb` lists the boards and how many posts each has.
- `+bb <board>` lists a board's posts, and `+bb <board>/<post>` reads one.
- `+bb/post <board>=<title>` writes a post, and `+bb/reply <board>/<post>` replies to one.
- `+bb/edit <board>/<post>` edits your own post.
- `+bb/subscribe <board>` tells you about new posts when you log in.

Boards can be named or numbered: `+bb 2/5` and `+bb General/5` work alike. Post numbers belong to each board.

## In-character boards

Boards marked **[IC]** hold in-character writing: letters, notices nailed to a door, reports. Your game may reward IC posts with XP, as it would a scene.

## Edits and history

Editing a post keeps every earlier version. Staff can archive a post, which takes it off the board without deleting it.

## On the website

The website shows each board's newest post and, when you're logged in, how many you haven't read. Reading a board on the website marks it read there, but it doesn't use up the notice you'd get in game when you log in.
