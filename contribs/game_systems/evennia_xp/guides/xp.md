XP is paid once a week, automatically, for the things your game rewards: roleplay sessions, concluded storylines, in-character writing and so on. You don't apply for it or claim it.

- `+xp` shows your balance, plus what you've earned since the last payout that hasn't been paid yet.
- `+xp/log` lists your recent awards.
- `+xp/sources` lists what currently earns XP in your game.

The website has the same on its XP page, with a breakdown by source.

## When it's paid

The weekly payout adds up everything you did since the last one. The next time you log in afterwards, you'll see a short summary of what each source paid. A week with nothing to pay shows nothing.

`+xp` shows what you've earned so far this week before it's paid. It's an estimate: staff review can still take a flagged item out before the payout.

:::insight[Paid for what happened, once]
Each award is tied to the thing that earned it, a session or a storyline, so it can only be paid once, even if the payout runs twice. That's why the estimate and the real payout agree, apart from anything staff set aside.
:::

## What changes the amount

Your game decides what each source is worth. Some games also scale XP for a while, during a quiet stretch between stories, say. `+xp` says so while XP is reduced, and `+xp/sources` and your game's own pages say what applies.

Staff can also grant XP by hand, with a reason that shows in your log.

## Spending it

XP is spent through other systems, such as buying and upgrading abilities on your character sheet. A purchase that's refunded gives back exactly what it cost.
