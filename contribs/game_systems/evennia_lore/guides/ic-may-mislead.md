---
key: ic-may-mislead
kind: principle
title: The IC layer may mislead; the OOC layer never does
summary: Characters can be fooled by what they read or hear. Players never are.
---
Everything in the game has an in-character side and an out-of-character side. The in-character side may be false: a rumor, a legend, a forged mark, a lie told well. The out-of-character side is always true: OOC notes, accuracy, who really made something, who is really playing a character.

Characters can be deceived, and that makes for good stories. Players can always find out what's actually going on.
