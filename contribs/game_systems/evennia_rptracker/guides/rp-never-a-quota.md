---
key: rp-never-a-quota
kind: principle
title: Roleplay is never a quota
summary: Playing is rewarded as it happens. There are no targets to meet and nothing to miss out on.
---
Roleplay earns its reward by happening, not by reaching a target. There's no number of scenes per week to hit, no sign-off to chase, and nothing lost for a week you couldn't play. Playing more brings more reward, but playing less is never a penalty.
