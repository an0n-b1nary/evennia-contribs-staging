Social is how players find each other and get together. Staff see more of it than players do, and can move people and things around. Players' side of it is on [[social]].

## Tasks and commands

- **Seeing who's where:** `+where` and the live map show staff every connected character, including unfindable ones, with a marker. `+finger <character>` adds a staff section with the account behind the character and its sessions.
- **Going dark:** `+unfindable dark` hides a staff character from `+where`, the map and `who`. Players can be unfindable but can't go dark. Anyone in the same room still sees you.
- **Travel:** `@tel <room>` takes staff anywhere, including private and secret rooms. `@tel <object>=<room>` moves a character or an object, which only staff can do.
- **Staff rooms:** rooms whose type is "staff" are left out of players' `+where`, `+hangouts`, `+summon`, `+join` and `+home`.
- **Rooms:** `+roomconfig/hangout <type>` lists a room under `+hangouts`, and `+roomconfig/smell` and `/sound` set its sensory lines. Staff can configure any room; players only rooms they own.
- **Mood:** `+mood <text>` sets a room's atmosphere line, and `+mood/clear` removes it. Staff can always set it. Players can in a room they own, or during a scene there: any participant in a public scene, or the host of any other.

## What players can and can't do to staff

- **Ignore:** a player's `+ignore` doesn't stop pages or summon requests from staff.
- **Staff-run marker:** a game can mark staff-run characters as "(staff-run)" in `+finger`, `who` and `+where`. It shows only that a character is staff-run, never which account runs it or what its other characters are.

## Privacy

- **Unfindable is a player's choice.** You can see through it to do your job; don't tell other players where an unfindable character is.
- **Accounts and alts** appear only in the staff section of `+finger`. Keep which characters share an account within staff.

## What's safe to change

Teleporting a character moves them without asking. Prefer `+summon` and `+join`, which ask first, unless there's a reason not to. A room's type, hangout and mood are ordinary room settings and safe to change.

## Review jobs

Social files no jobs.
