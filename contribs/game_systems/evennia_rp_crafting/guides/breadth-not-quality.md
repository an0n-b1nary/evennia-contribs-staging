---
key: breadth-not-quality
kind: principle
title: Breadth, never quality
summary: Crafting opens more kinds of thing to make. It never makes one thing better than another.
---
A crafting system with quality tiers rewards whoever has played longest and asks newcomers to make worse things first. So a craft gives you breadth instead: more kinds of thing you can make, in more styles. Your first item is as good as anyone's, and nothing anyone makes changes a check, so what makes an item stand out is what its maker wrote.
