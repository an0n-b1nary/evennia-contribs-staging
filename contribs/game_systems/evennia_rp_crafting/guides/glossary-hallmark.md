title: Hallmark
requires: RP_CRAFTING_REVEALED
summary: The OOC line on a crafted item that says who made it, in which crafts, and in which Pattern.
---
The **Craft hallmark (OOC)** line on every crafted item, such as "Made by Morgan (Weaver)". It comes from the game's record of the craft, so it can't be edited, and it travels with the item. See [[crafting]].
