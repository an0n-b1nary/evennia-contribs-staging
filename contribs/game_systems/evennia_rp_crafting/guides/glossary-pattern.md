title: Pattern
requires: [RP_CRAFTING_REVEALED, RP_CRAFTING_PATTERNS_REVEALED]
summary: Crafting knowledge a character learns and teaches. It names itself in an item's hallmark and may open new kinds of item.
---
A recipe, method or style a crafter knows. It's learnt in the story or from a teacher in a shared scene, named in the hallmark of items worked in it, and never makes an item better. See [[patterns]].
