A **companion** is a crafted creature or contraption that goes about with your character: a clockwork bird, a small dog, a lantern that bobs along behind. It's for the story. It has no stats, carries nothing and never fights.

- `+pet` lists your companions.
- `+pet/follow <companion>` has it follow you, and `+pet/stay <companion>` leaves it where it is.
- `+pet/pose <companion>=<text>` poses for it, in your words.
- `+pet/trick <companion>=<trick>` has it do one of its tricks.

## Following

A following companion takes the same way you went, through ordinary exits, and goes where it's allowed to. If it can't follow, it stays behind and you're told. To move it somewhere else, visit or carry it.

## What it does

Everything a companion does comes from you or from what its maker wrote. `+pet/pose` is your own writing, so it counts as a pose. A companion may also have an arrival line when it comes into a room, tricks, if your game has opened things that can be used again, and an ambient line now and then. None of those count as posing or roleplay, and they follow other players' choices to mute effects. Ambient lines pause while you're offline.

:::insight[A companion is yours, not a character]
A companion only does what you or its maker wrote, and nothing it does on its own counts as roleplay. A creature that acts and speaks for itself is a non-player character, run by people, not by a crafted item.
:::

Someone who ignores you doesn't see your companion or its lines.

## Giving it away

Pick a companion up before giving or selling it. Once it changes hands it belongs to the new owner, and it starts out staying put.
