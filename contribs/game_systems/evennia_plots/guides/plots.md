A **plot thread** is a storyline that players run, anything from a feud between two families to a smuggling ring. A thread ties together the scenes, events and posts that tell its story, so anyone can follow it from start to finish. Staff group threads into larger [[glossary-arc|arcs]].

- `+plot` lists active public threads, and `+plot <number>` shows one.
- `+plot/create <name>=<tagline>` proposes a thread, and `+plot/activate <number>` starts it.
- `+plot/update <number>` adds an in-character update to the thread's story.
- `+plot/link/scene <number>=<scene>` ties a scene to the thread. `/link/event` and `/link/post` do the same for calendar events and board posts.
- `+plot/conclude <number>` ends it.

## Privacy

| Privacy | Who can read it | Who can link things to it |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Anyone | Anyone, while it's active |
| **Invite-only** | Anyone | Invited characters and staff |
| **Private** | Its creator and staff | Its creator and staff |

`+plot/privacy` sets it, and `+plot/invite` invites someone. `+plot/follow` keeps you posted on a thread you're not running.

## Concluding a thread

When a thread concludes, everyone who took part gets a small XP bonus, if your game uses XP. The bonus grows with how the story was run:

- **Scheduling ahead** counts the most: an event on the calendar, linked to the thread, with good notice.
- **More than one scene** linked to the thread adds a little.
- **An in-character board post** linked to it adds a little more.

A thread concluded very soon after it was started earns no bonus.

:::insight[Rewarding the habits that include people]
The bonus doesn't reward winning, or even finishing quickly. It rewards things that let other people join in: notice, more than one session, and a written account anyone can read.
:::

## Arcs

Staff group threads into **arcs**, the larger stories of the game. One arc at a time can be the **current** arc. Some arcs are **downtime** arcs, a breather between stories, and your game may pay less XP or none while one is current. Play carries on as usual during downtime.
