A [[glossary-region|region]] is a named area of the world, such as a district, a valley or a stretch of coast. Staff draw the regions, and each room can belong to one or more of them.

- `+region` lists the regions.
- `+region/view <name>` shows one, with its description and rooms.
- `+region/here` shows the region or regions of the room you're in.

The website has a page for each region, and one for each room listing its regions and, if your game has a map, where the room is on it. A room's page can also show details other systems add, such as who built the place.

## One room, several regions

Regions can overlap. A temple might be part of a city and of a sacred valley at once. One of a room's regions is its **primary** region, the one shown wherever there's only room for one name, such as a map tooltip.

Other systems can use regions too. Lore can belong to a region, for example, so stories about a place sit with the place.

## Hidden rooms

Rooms hidden from you, such as staff areas, secret places and private homes you aren't allowed into, are left out of a region's room list and have no page you can open. A region's page never gives away that a hidden room exists, and a room's map link is left off when that part of the map is private.
