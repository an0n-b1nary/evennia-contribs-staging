---
key: time-pays-quantity
kind: principle
title: Time pays quantity, choices shape composition
summary: How much you get depends on time passing; what you get depends on your choices.
---
Income that grows with time played rewards whoever can play most, and punishes everyone else for having a life. So a regular income comes from time passing, the same for everyone. Choices still matter, but they shape *what* you get, not *how much*. That keeps a character's interests visible in what they hold, without turning them into a grind.
