Each week your character gathers a share of resources, the raw materials your game uses for making things. You don't have to go anywhere or do anything to earn them. What you can choose is which resources they lean towards.

- `+resources` shows what you hold, the cap on weekly gathering, and what you gained last week.
- `+resources/catalog` lists the resources that can be gathered now.
- `+gather <resource or category>` leans your gathering towards it, and `+gather/clear` stops.
- `+gather` on its own shows your lean and what's open to gather.

## Weekly gathering

Gathering happens on its own once a week, while you're away as well as while you play. It doesn't depend on roleplay, on how long you were logged in, or on who you played with. Everyone gathers the same amount until their stores are nearly full; what differs is the mix.

When you next log in, a short line names what you gained. A week that adds nothing says nothing.

:::insight[Time pays quantity, choices shape composition]{time-pays-quantity}
How much you gather depends only on time passing, so nobody falls behind for playing less or gets ahead by grinding. Your choices decide what kind of resources you get, which is where your character's interests show.
:::

## Choosing a lean

Without a lean, you gather from everything that's open, at random, with some resources commoner than others. A **lean** names one resource or a whole category, and makes those likelier to come up. It never changes how much you gather. Your lean stays until you change it, and if what you lean towards can't be gathered right now, `+gather` says so.

What's open depends on the world, not on where you stand. Resources tied to parts of the world that haven't opened yet aren't offered, and a common pool is always open. If your game has a map, a room's panel can show the kinds of resource found in its terrain, and whether your lean matches one. Your profile may show your lean too.

## The cap

Your stores have a cap on weekly gathering. As you near it, you gather less, and at the cap you gather nothing more; the login line tells you once when your stores are full. Trades, rewards and anything staff give you can take you past the cap. Some things you own, such as a workshop, can raise it.

:::insight[Nothing decays]{nothing-decays}
What you've gathered stays yours however long you're away. The cap only slows gathering for full stores; it never takes anything back.
:::

A resource your game retires stays in your stores, and you can still trade or spend it. It just stops being gathered.

## Plot rewards

A plot can promise its participants resources when it concludes. The plot's owners propose a reward for each participant, and staff approve it. When the plot concludes, every active participant receives the full reward, including anyone who's offline. Running or owning the plot alone doesn't count; you have to have taken part. The reward ignores your lean and the cap, and you'll see it the next time you log in.

## Spending and trading

Resources are spent by other systems, such as making things, if your game has them. If your game has trading, you can trade and give resources like anything else, by name and amount, such as `3 Grain`.

Guests don't gather resources or receive plot rewards.
