---
key: nothing-decays
kind: principle
title: Nothing decays
summary: What you've earned stays yours while you're away. Caps slow income; they never take anything back.
---
Nothing you've earned rots, rusts or drains away while you're not playing. A cap may stop income piling up forever, but it only slows what comes in. Nobody has to log in to protect what they have, and a break from the game never costs you anything you already hold.
