kind: term
title: Grade
summary: How good a stat is, on the game's ladder of grades. Higher is better.
---
How good one of a character's stats is. Grades form a ladder, and the game names the steps: letter grades are common, but ranks or titles work too. [[glossary-pip|Pips]] fine-tune a stat within its grade. See [[checks]].
