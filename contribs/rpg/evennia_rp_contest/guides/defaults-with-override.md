---
key: defaults-with-override
kind: principle
title: Sensible defaults, always overridable
summary: The system suggests the obvious choice, and you can always choose something else.
---
Where there's an obvious choice, the system offers it, so you don't have to work it out every time. It never makes it the only choice. You can always do it your own way, and the people you're playing with can see that you did.
