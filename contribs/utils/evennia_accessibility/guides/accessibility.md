These settings are stored on your account, so they apply to every character you play and every time you log in.

- `+screenreader/on` turns on screen-reader mode, and `+screenreader/off` turns it off. `+sr` is short for it.
- `+ambient/mute` mutes effects that reach you from other rooms or ambient channels, and `+ambient/unmute` brings them back.

## Screen-reader mode

Commands that support it swap tables and colour-only markers for plain lists and words that read well aloud, with tools such as NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver. A status shown only as a colour becomes a word, and a decorative symbol becomes its meaning or disappears.

:::insight[Colour is never the only signal]
These tools pair every colour cue with a word or a symbol, whether or not screen-reader mode is on, so meaning never rests on colour alone. That helps people who don't see colour well, or who play on a client that doesn't show it.
:::

## Muting distant effects

Some games let objects or abilities create effects that reach beyond the room: a bell heard across town, a storm on a channel. `+ambient/mute` hides those. Effects used in the room you're in still show, because they're part of the scene in front of you.

## On the website

Website forms built with these tools label every field, name every error next to it, and, when a form posts as one of your characters, say which character. Keyboard focus is always shown with a clear outline, and motion is reduced if your device asks for it.
