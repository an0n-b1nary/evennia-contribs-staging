Every character gets a weekly allowance of coins, paid while they're away as well as while they play. A purse that's nearly full fills more slowly, and nothing in it decays.

- `+balance` shows your purse.
- `give 10 coins to <character>` hands coins over.
- `+offer <character>=<what you give> for <what you want>` proposes a trade, and `+accept` takes one.

:::insight[Both sides or neither]{penalize-dont-forbid}
A trade moves everything at once or nothing at all, so no one can be left holding half of a deal.
:::

The page stays hidden until `RP_ECONOMY_REVEALED` is on. In this sandbox it's on from the start.
